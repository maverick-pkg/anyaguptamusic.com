04 The Artist

About

photo

ANYA writes pop songs the way other people keep diaries — late at night, in real time, with nothing left out. Raised between small-town basements and borrowed studio hours, she taught herself production at fifteen and spent the next few years turning voice memos into the kind of hooks that stay with you for days.

Her breakout singles earned early tastemaker support and a fast-growing audience that treats every release like a group chat come to life. With her new single "Slow Motion" — a moody, cinematic step into her next era — she's headed out on her first headline run across the U.S. this fall.

— ANYA
