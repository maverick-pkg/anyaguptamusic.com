About

ANYA writes the kind of pop songs that feel like reading a diary you were handed on purpose — bright, blunt, and a little bit heartbroken. Raised on her mom’s mixtapes and a secondhand keyboard, she started posting bedroom demos at fifteen and quietly never stopped, trading polish for honesty until the honesty became the polish.

Her new single “Slow Motion” takes the smallest moment — a hallway, a half-finished sentence — and stretches it until it aches. After a year of opening slots, borrowed vans, and rooms that kept selling out anyway, this fall the rooms get bigger. Bring a friend; she’ll probably write a song about it.
