About Anya

Anya Gupta is a fifteen-year-old singer-songwriter from Tennessee whose music celebrates self-love, individuality, and the light we all carry within us.

“For me, songwriting is a way to clear my mind. When I write about the challenges I face as a teenager, it helps me understand them and move through them.”

She began studying voice at age six and has spent nearly a decade performing — honing her songwriting at Langston Studios and earning finalist honors in The Factor and Delta Songwriting Star. Her debut album Becoming arrives August 17: seven songs, including the singles “Tat Twam Asi,” “Awaken the Lights,” and “You’re Original,” inviting listeners to celebrate who they are and let their inner light shine.
