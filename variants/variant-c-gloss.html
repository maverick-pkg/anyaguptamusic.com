About

Anya

ANYA writes pop songs that feel like secrets told at 2 a.m. — glossy on the surface, raw underneath. Raised on a steady diet of radio ballads and her mother's record collection, she started uploading bedroom demos at sixteen and hasn't stopped since. Her breakout EP turned late-night heartbreak into something you could dance to, earning early praise from tastemakers and a fiercely loyal following.

With her new single "Slow Motion," ANYA steps fully into her own light: bigger hooks, sharper edges, and a voice that refuses to blur into the background. It's the sound of an artist done waiting for permission — and this is only the beginning.
